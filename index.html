Selected research

Projects with a question, a method and a result.

Linear-ramp and warm-start QAOA for trajectory control of multi-actuator soft catheters

Undergraduate thesis · Supervisor: Dr. Mohamed Gaber · 2025–2026

Trajectory tracking for a multi-actuator soft catheter is cast as a QUBO problem, with the reduction checked numerically. On a 24-qubit statevector simulation I benchmark classical MPC, simulated annealing, full-parameter QAOA, linear-ramp QAOA and warm-start QAOA, then extend the scheme to closed-loop receding-horizon control under hysteresis.

Simulation study; the actuator model is not experimentally calibrated.

Adsorption of HgO on Au(111): a dual machine-learning force-field benchmark

JINR, INTEREST programme (remote) · Dr. Dipayan Sen, Dr. Miroslav Iliaš · 2026

CHGNet and MACE-MP-0 across the four high-symmetry sites of a 4×4×5 Au(111) slab. The bridge site has the lowest adsorption energy (−2.04 eV), but the 75 meV spread between sites is smaller than the ensemble uncertainty (about 216 meV), so no site is statistically resolved. The two models differ by 601 meV at the on-top site. Vibrational, free-energy and Langevin MD analyses complete the study.

APEX: a multifunctional protective screen for solar panels in extreme heat

With Prof. Ayman El-Amin · 2023–2024

A transparent multilayer screen combining a luminescent layer, integrated thermoelectric harvesting and a self-cleaning anti-reflection nanocoating, modelled in COMSOL for Aswan's 60 °C+ conditions.
