Why physics

It began in preparatory school with a lesson on the atom, and a Russian nuclear-physics textbook my brother brought home. When scholarships in other fields were on offer, I chose pure physics.

Quantum mechanics led me to quantum computing, and then to the question that shapes my work: what does it take for quantum technology to work outside a simulation? That means noise, materials, control and honest uncertainty.
